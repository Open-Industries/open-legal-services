# Domain Standards — Legal Services

Alignment mapping for this core. *Aligned to* throughout: nothing here is legal advice,
and professional-conduct duties (privilege, conflicts, trust accounting rules) remain
governed by each adopter's bar and jurisdiction.

## Concept mapping

| Platform concept | Domain standard / framework | Mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Document metadata | Akoma Ntoso (LegalDocML) | Document catalogue follows AKN naming/identifier discipline for metadata |
| Signatures | eIDAS | Executed by qualified trust service providers at the boundary; this core records status only |
| Client funds | Trust-accounting practice | Guarded retainer accounts; the no-overrun invariant; append-only charges |
| Deadlines | Procedural rules of the jurisdiction | Deadline rules context with reasoned decisions; jurisdictions supply the calendars |

## Scope boundaries

No document content, no legal advice, no court filing: the core is the practice platform
(matters, retainers, deadlines, document metadata) around professional work.
